From detection to early warning.

The platform is designed around one clear workflow: identify an approaching vehicle, evaluate its movement, and surface a warning when the monitored speed threshold is crossed.

◉

Detect & Track

Vehicles can be represented through camera/video input, mapped monitoring data, or simulated sensor data for a reliable college demonstration.

⌁

Estimate Movement

The monitoring workflow can display vehicle speed, distance from the monitored point, and estimated arrival time.

⚠

Warn Early

Vehicles crossing the project's hyper-speed threshold can trigger a visible warning and become part of the event history.
